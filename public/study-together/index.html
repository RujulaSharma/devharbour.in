Online study timer for friends

Run a solo focus timer or invite friends to a private Dev Harbour study group.

Open the focus timer →

Start a solo session

Open the timer, select Countdown, and choose a 25-minute focus block. Pick one task you can check at the end, such as solving a practice problem or reviewing ten flashcards. Start the countdown and take a short break after the block.

Study with a friend

  1. Sign in on the timer page and open Study Together.
  2. Choose Create, name your private group, and copy the invite link.
  3. Send the invite to a friend. They can sign in and join from that link.
  4. Start a focus session. Group members can see when you are studying or on a break.

You can hide your study status or mute group alerts. The optional subject field is private. Use the group to agree on a time and check in, then work on your own tasks.

Try a 25-minute pair session

Take one minute to tell each other what you plan to finish. Work independently for the focus block, then share a result and one thing you will try next. For a concrete coding example, read the focused development session guide.
