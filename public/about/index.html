About Dev Harbour

What Dev Harbour provides and how to use its learning and building tools.

Dev Harbour is a developer learning and project community founded by Rujula Sharma. It brings learning resources, browser-based coding practice, a project gallery, focus tools, and hackathon information together in one place.

Start with a task

Read a public guide, try its exercise, and use the workspace to record what you learned. Browse the project gallery to see ideas and follow their published demo links. Sign in to save supported account activity or submit your own project.

What works today

The platform offers learning tracks, browser sample execution, account-based project submissions, workspace notes and tasks, a focus timer, and event-specific certificate access. Official server grading for coding challenges is currently unavailable; browser samples are not a substitute for hidden-test evaluation. Some community navigation options link to existing tools rather than separate services.

Certificates and events

Eligibility depends on the event registration list and published event rules. Certificates record participation or the stated achievement; they are not accredited degrees or employment guarantees. Third-party event links lead to their organisers, whose dates, fees, and rules should be checked before registering.

Our publishing approach

Our public guides explain a concrete task with examples, limitations, and exercises. They are prepared for Dev Harbour with AI-assisted drafting and editorial checks; corrections are welcome. External documentation and submitted projects remain the work of their respective authors. We do not claim affiliation with a resource provider merely because we link to its documentation.
