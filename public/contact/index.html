Contact Dev Harbour

Contact the team about accounts, corrections, project content, privacy, and certificates.

Email sharmarujula123@gmail.com for Dev Harbour support. The site is operated by Rujula Sharma and the Dev Harbour team.

Account and certificate help

Include the affected page URL, the event name if relevant, the time of the issue, and a short description of what happened. Contact us from the email used to register when possible. Never send passwords, OTPs, session tokens, database connection strings, or payment-card details.

Corrections and content reports

Tell us which paragraph, project, or link is incorrect and what evidence supports a correction. For copyright reports, identify the original work, the URL on Dev Harbour, your relationship to the rights holder, and a reply address. We assess reports and can restrict content while reviewing them.

Privacy requests

Use the same email for account exports, corrections, or deletion requests. We may ask you to verify account ownership. Read the privacy policy for the data categories we process.

Community

Visit our LinkedIn page or WhatsApp community. Messages on third-party platforms are subject to those services' privacy practices. There is no guaranteed support response time.
