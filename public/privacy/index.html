Privacy policy

How Dev Harbour uses account data, browser storage, analytics, and advertising services.

Updated November 24, 2026. Dev Harbour is operated by Rujula Sharma and the Dev Harbour team. For privacy requests, contact sharmarujula123@gmail.com.

Data we process

Registration stores your name, email, username, password hash, and profile details. Google sign-in supplies a verified email and permitted profile information. We store learning activity, project submissions, workspace records, and certificate eligibility information to provide those features. Hosting providers process request metadata such as IP addresses for delivery and abuse prevention.

Storage and service providers

Account and application records are stored in Supabase PostgreSQL. The website and API use hosting infrastructure including Cloudflare and Render. Resend delivers verification and recovery emails. Google provides sign-in and, when enabled with the relevant choices, analytics and advertising. Providers may process information outside your country. Credentials remain server-side; passwords are stored as hashes rather than readable passwords.

Cookies and browser storage

Browser storage retains sign-in sessions, preferences, and local task or timer state. Sign out on shared devices. Our optional Google Analytics integration is off until you opt in using Cookie settings. You can change that choice and remove analytics cookies through that control or your browser. Essential account functions continue to work if you decline analytics.

Advertising

Dev Harbour is applying to Google AdSense. Ad serving is not enabled by this application alone. If Google ads are enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies, local storage, IP addresses, and identifiers to deliver, measure, and, with applicable consent, personalise ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies based on prior visits to this or other websites. Google's advertising cookies can enable Google and partners to serve ads based on visits to this and other sites. You can manage personalised advertising at My Ad Center; learn how Google uses data at Google's partner-site policy. Required regional advertising choices must be provided before ads are enabled.

Public content and certificates

Project descriptions and public profiles may be visible to other visitors. Do not submit private information in public fields. Certificate verification exposes limited credential details; information shown varies by event. Innovative India eligibility is checked against the registration email, with Google sign-in required for Gmail claims.

Retention and your choices

We retain account records while needed to provide the service, protect it from abuse, or maintain legitimate certificate records. Request access, correction, export, or deletion by emailing us from your account email. We may verify ownership before acting and explain any records that need to be retained. No fixed deletion deadline or legal-compliance certification is promised here.

Changes and younger users

This service is intended for students and developers and is not designed to collect information from children under 13. Contact us if a child's data was submitted. We will update this page when our practices change.
